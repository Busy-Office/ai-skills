# Run review — sample-app

The loop has been running for a couple of weeks now and overall the picture is
reasonably encouraging, though there are certainly some areas where things
could be improved if the team wanted to invest a bit of effort in tuning how
the whole arrangement operates day to day. Broadly speaking the runs seem to be
doing what they were designed to do, and the commits that come out of them are
mostly reasonable in size and scope, which is a good sign overall. There is a
fair amount of token spend involved, and some of that spend is probably not
buying very much, although it is difficult to be precise about exactly how much
without doing a deeper analysis than was possible here. The team may want to
consider whether the current arrangement is the best use of the budget, and
whether some of the work might be better handled by different agents. It is
also worth thinking about whether the backlog items are specified clearly
enough for the loop to make progress on them without getting stuck, since a
few of the runs did appear to spend quite a lot of effort without producing
much in the way of visible output at the end of the session.
