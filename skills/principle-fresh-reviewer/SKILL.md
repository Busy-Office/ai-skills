# Fresh reviewer

**Apply when** a change, plan, report or claim is about to be judged: a review round, a verifier, a
check before the owner sees it.

**The rule.**

- **The reviewer did not build it.** Another model where one is available, otherwise a fresh
  context. Never the builder grading its own work, and never a context that already holds the
  builder's reasons.
- **Hand it the work, not your view of it:** the diff or the artifact, the goal, and the owner's
  decisions verbatim. A reviewer handed a hypothesis tends to return it confirmed.
- **It reports; it does not fix.** A fix is the next round, by a fresh fixer, and the fix gets its
  own review.
- **Wide or risky change:** two reviewers on different models. Different reviewers catch different
  real bugs; merge their findings, do not average them.

**Why.** The builder's context holds every reason the work looks right. A fresh reader sees only
what is on the page, which is all the next reader will see.

**The sign you skipped it.** "I reviewed my changes and they look good"; a reviewer briefed with the
builder's summary; one agent fixing and approving in the same turn.
