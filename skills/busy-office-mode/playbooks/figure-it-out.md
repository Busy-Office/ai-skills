### Figure it out

**No playbook fits. Write the one-off playbook before doing the work.**

1. Say in one line why none of the playbooks fits.
2. Write a short playbook in the same shape: a stance line, numbered steps that each end on a
   checkable result and name the skill they call, and a reply line. Borrow steps from the nearest
   playbooks before inventing new ones.
3. Open it as the todo list, then work it.
4. The same kind of task comes up again → propose it as a permanent playbook
   (`principle-encode-lessons-in-structure`).

**Reply:** the playbook you wrote, what it produced, and whether it should become permanent.
