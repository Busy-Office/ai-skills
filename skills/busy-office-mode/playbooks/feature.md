### Feature

**You own the design. Delegate the build; verify every claim.**

1. Open a worktree and a branch for the batch (`solo-flow`). Write the gate and the budget
   (`improve-until-done`).
2. If how the affected part works is not already clear, `how` first.
3. Brief one builder (`deploy-agents`): the goal, the worktree, the owner's decisions verbatim, the
   targeted tests, a graded report.
4. Re-run its Ran claims and open its Read citations, yourself or by a cheap agent
   (`principle-prove-it-works`). A claim that does not reproduce is a finding.
5. Fresh review of the diff against the goal and the owner's decisions (`review`).
6. Fix rounds: a fresh fixer per round, briefed with the open findings, until a stop rule holds
   (`improve-until-done`).
7. Final check: the full suite, run alone, by you (`principle-guard-the-machine`).
8. Explain the batch — the repository's own step, if it has one (e.g. an `explain-landing` skill);
   otherwise a short message: what changed, why, the evidence with its grades, what is open. Ask one
   yes/no about that exact version (`principle-decide-at-the-right-level`).
9. Land only after the yes (`landing`).

**Reply:** what changed, the evidence with its grades, what is still open.
