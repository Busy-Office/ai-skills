### Feature

**You own the design. Delegate the build; verify every claim.**

1. **Worktree.** Open a worktree and a branch for the batch (`solo-flow`). Write the gate and the
   budget (`improve-until-done`).
2. **How.** If how the affected part works is not already clear, `busy-office:how` first.
3. **Build.** Brief one builder (`deploy-agents`): the goal, the worktree, the owner's decisions
   verbatim, the targeted tests, a graded report.
4. **Verify claims.** Re-run its Ran claims and open its Read citations, yourself or by a cheap
   agent (`principle-prove-it-works`). A claim that does not reproduce is a finding.
5. **Review.** A fresh review of the diff against the goal and the owner's decisions
   (`busy-office:review`).
6. **Fix rounds.** A fresh fixer per round, briefed with the open findings, until a stop rule holds
   (`improve-until-done`).
7. **Final check.** The full suite, run alone, by you, once per batch
   (`principle-guard-the-machine`).
8. **Explain.** The repository's own step, if it has one (e.g. an `explain-landing` skill);
   otherwise a short message: what changed, why, the evidence with its grades, what is open.
9. **Land** through `landing`: squash and rebase, one yes/no about that exact tip, then land.

**Reply:** what changed, the evidence with its grades, what is still open.
